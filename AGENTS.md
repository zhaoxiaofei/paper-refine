This repository MUST satisfy these constraints.

1. **Judgment is blind to provenance.** A judge sees the manuscript as an artifact, not as a product. It must not see which agent, arm, round, prompt, or prior session produced a version, and it must not see another session’s findings or discovery list. Identity in the panel is an opaque content token. External facts needed to score the paper — raw data, code, and admitted reviewer concerns — may appear only as anonymized evidence, never as a label of which candidate is which.

2. **The thing that was scored is the thing that wins.** A candidate’s identity is its content digest. The champion pin, the next round’s base, and the published package are that digest’s bytes, not a later “cleaned up” cousin. Content-identical candidates are one candidate. A version that was not judged cannot win. Tracking, redlines, PDF renders, and post-hoc relabeling of the ledger are not allowed to move a pin, a score, or an input manifest.

3. **Facts are not a revision surface.** The operator’s source, the pristine copy, raw data, and external review feedback are read-only. The manuscript is brought into agreement with them; they are never edited, dropped, or extended so that the manuscript looks right. They are not author prose, not a word-count surface, and not a scored improvement. When two sources disagree, the higher producer wins: code, then raw data, then figures, then tables, then main text, then supplementary text.

4. **No rule the venue did not state.** A venue is a recorded rule set. A journal name selects nothing. An article type is measured only against its own limits. If a limit, caption rule, section, or template requirement is absent, the pipeline counts and names the gap. It does not hard-code a journal, borrow another type’s numbers, copy exemplar prose, or let an agent invent a requirement. An invented rule is a defect in the pipeline, not a defect in the paper.

5. **There is one ranking function, and it is pure.** Every scored defect lands in the same six tiers — correctness, preservation, completeness, consistency, writing, formatting — and the same four severities. A newly noticed phenomenon is mapped into that scale; it does not become a seventh ranked class. Given the panel’s own ledger and the digests, the champion is a pure function: cumulative defect prefix, then median, mean, IQR, digest, and the incumbent stays on an exact statistic tie. Self-reported totals, conflict notes, and a defect audit may annotate that result. They may not replace its inputs.

6. **No role grades its own homework.** The session that writes a version does not score it. The session that audits scores does not select the champion. A repair of bookkeeping does not edit the manuscript. In a concern-scoped revision, changes stay inside the admitted concerns; an unscoped rewrite is a different mode, not a quiet upgrade of this one. The no-feedback path must stay the no-feedback path: adding a journal mode must not change what a root does when no journal mode is on.

7. **Output is not certification.** A `final_clean_version/` always being written does not mean the decision passed. Certified means the panel was complete, the champion re-derives from the sheets, the evidence is unchanged, and the enabled gates are clean. A skipped integrity check is reported as skipped. Provisional and blocked results stay labeled and are never presented as a signed answer.
